# IV Lens privacy policy

*Plum Comet Labs · last updated 28 October 2026*

**In short: IV Lens doesn't collect, keep or share any personal data. It has no internet access,
so nothing it sees can leave your phone.**

## What IV Lens sees

- **Your screen, only while the bubble is on.** You start it in the app, and Android asks you to
  allow screen capture first. IV Lens reads each image on the phone to find the Pokémon's numbers
  (CP, HP, stardust cost, appraisal bars), then lets it go. Images are never saved and never sent.
- **Text recognition** is Google's ML Kit, built into the app and running on the phone. IV Lens has
  no internet access, so ML Kit can't send anything either.

## What IV Lens keeps

- **Your saved Pokémon and your settings**, in the app's own storage on your phone. Uninstalling
  the app deletes them.
- **Export** writes your saved list to a file you choose. Where that file goes is up to you.
- If your phone backs itself up (Android's backup), Android may include the app's storage in your
  own backup. That's handled by Android under Google's terms; we never see it.

## What you can send us

- **Report a bug** opens a form where you write what went wrong. It shows what's added to it: the
  app version, the phone model, the Android version, the screen size and, from a result card, what
  IV Lens read. You then copy it to our Discord or share it another way; nothing is sent by the
  app itself. We only see what you post or send.
- **Links** (Discord, our website, Pokémon pages on pokemon.gameinfo.io) open in the Discord app or
  your browser, under their own privacy policies.

## No ads, no accounts, no tracking

IV Lens has no ads, no purchases, no sign-in, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

## Children

IV Lens isn't made for children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with its date.
