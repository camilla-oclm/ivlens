Hi! I'm looking for testers for my closed test, and I'll test your app back for the full 14 days.

**The app:** IV Lens is a free IV checker for Pokémon GO. A small bubble over the game reads the Pokémon on screen and gives you its IVs, PvP ranks and a verdict: keep it, use it for PvP or raids, or transfer it. It has no ads, no account and no internet access.

**Heads-up: it captures your screen.** That's how it reads the Pokémon, so here's exactly what that means:

- It asks for two permissions: **screen capture** (to read the game) and **display over other apps** (for the bubble).
- Android's screen-capture prompt warns that the app can see everything on your screen. That's the standard warning for any app that captures the screen.
- It captures the screen **only while the bubble is on**. You start the bubble in the app, Android shows a notification while it runs, and a long-press on the bubble stops it.
- Each image is read **on your phone** and then dropped. Nothing is saved, and nothing is sent: the app has no internet access at all.
- It never taps or plays for you. It only reads.
- Privacy policy: https://plumcometlabs.com/ivlens/privacy

**Pokémon GO players especially welcome:** the app only really works if you play Pokémon GO (in English). If you don't play, opening it now and then is fine. If you do play, I'd love your feedback. That's worth more to me than the swap, and there's a Report a bug button in the app.
